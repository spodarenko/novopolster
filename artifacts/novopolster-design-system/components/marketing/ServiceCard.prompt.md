Service tile for the services grid — icon, title, one-line description, transparent "from" price.

```jsx
<ServiceCard
  icon="assets/icons/sofa.svg"
  title="Sofa-Reinigung"
  description="Tiefenreinigung für Stoff & Leder, vor Ort."
  priceFrom="89 €"
/>
```

The `priceFrom` line is the brand's core differentiator (transparent pricing shown upfront, unlike competitors who hide it behind a contact form) — always include it when the price is known.
